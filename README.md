# Thomas Schwarzfischer

I'm an engineer working at the intersection of **machine learning, computer vision, and robotics**. I recently completed an M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Technical University of Munich, specializing in automation and robotics.

I enjoy taking ideas from models and experiments all the way to systems that can be tested in simulation or on real robots. During my work at BMW, I've been focused on end-to-end driving, closed-loop evaluation, and making learned driving policies more controllable.
