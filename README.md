# Hi, I'm Thomas 👋

I'm an engineer working at the intersection of **machine learning, robotics, and autonomous driving**. I recently completed an M.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the Technical University of Munich, specializing in automation and robotics.

I enjoy taking ideas from models and experiments all the way to systems that can be tested in simulation or on real robots. Lately, I've been focused on end-to-end driving, closed-loop evaluation, and making learned driving policies more controllable.

## Selected projects

- **[CLAP-AD](https://clap-ad.github.io)** — Closed-loop aware planning for end-to-end autonomous driving, exploring imitation-free speed scoring and controllable planning.
- **[Comfortably Stuck](https://comfortably-stuck.github.io)** — An investigation into evaluation pitfalls in Bench2Drive's closed-loop protocol.

## Research

- **[Closed-Loop Aware Planning via Imitation-Free Speed Scoring for End-to-End Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36079)** — WACV 2027, accepted; equal contribution.
- **[Comfortably Stuck: Evaluation Pitfalls in Bench2Drive's Closed-Loop Protocol](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21741)** — ICRA 2027, under review; equal contribution.
